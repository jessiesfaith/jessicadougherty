Cofounder & CEO Now
Scout Quest Education
Apr 2026 — Present · 5 mos · United States · Remote

I'm developing Scout Quest Education, an education-technology platform designed to break the mold of traditional classroom software by rethinking how students, teachers, parents, and AI interact inside the learning process.

Scout Quest is being built as a K–5 reading, writing, and AI-literacy platform that combines student engagement, teacher-controlled assignment workflows, adaptive practice, learning dashboards, and responsible AI support. The goal is not to replace teachers, but to give them better tools to understand student progress, recommend targeted assignments, approve AI-assisted learning adjustments, and support students at their actual learning level.

The platform is designed around a simple but important idea: education software should be engaging for students, useful for teachers, transparent for parents, and responsible from a privacy and compliance standpoint. Scout Quest begins with reading and writing skill development, then expands into AI-era learning pathways that include history, science, invention, digital workflow literacy, coding concepts, process thinking, and responsible use of AI tools.

As Cofounder & CEO, I lead product vision, early strategy, hiring, compliance planning, curriculum direction, technology roadmap, and go-to-market development — with a strong focus on student data privacy, teacher approval workflows, parent/guardian visibility, audit logs, accessibility, and California K–12 readiness.
