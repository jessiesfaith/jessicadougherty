User guide

Career master

This is the only place your career facts live. Everything else — templates, archived resumes, job matching — reads from here. Fix a typo once and every resume inherits it.

Editing

Type directly into any field. The top right shows Unsaved changes and the Save button lights up. Nothing is stored until you press Save.

If you try to leave with unsaved edits, your browser will warn you. That warning is the only thing standing between you and losing work — don't click through it out of habit.

Completed vs In progress

Every entry has a status dropdown. Completed entries group at the top of a section, in-progress below. Your CPA sits in progress until results land — flip the dropdown then, no retyping.

Reordering

  • ⤒ to the top of its group, ⤓ to the bottom
  • ▲ ▼ one position at a time
  • ⠿ grab and drag; an orange line shows where it will land

Reordering stays inside a group. Moving something within Completed never disturbs In progress. To move an entry between groups, use the status dropdown — dragging across the divider is ignored on purpose, so you can't change a status by slipping.

Adding and removing

+ Add at the top of a section creates a blank entry, marked in progress. + line adds a bullet. The bin deletes an entry — templates that referenced it just skip it, they don't break.

Resume builder

A template is a selection, not a copy. It stores which entries to include and in what order. Your actual words stay in Career master, so a fix there flows into every template automatically.

  1. New creates a template. Name it for the role — "Controller — public company".
  2. Set Target role. It replaces your headline at the top of that resume.
  3. Summary is optional. Leave it blank to use the one from Career master.
  4. Tick what belongs in this version. The preview updates as you go.
  5. Print / PDF opens a clean print view — use your browser's "Save as PDF".
Tailor with AI proposes bullet rewrites, one at a time, each with Accept or Reject. Nothing touches your master until you accept, and accepting still leaves you to press Save. It may only rephrase facts already in your master — it cannot invent numbers, employers, dates, or upgrade a credential. If a suggestion contains something you never wrote, reject it and tell me.

Archive

Save to archive freezes the resume exactly as it reads at that moment — the text, not a pointer to it. Edit your master afterwards and the archived copy does not move.

That is deliberate. Six months from now, "what exactly did I send them?" needs a real answer.

Each snapshot records the career-master version it came from. View opens it, Copy text gives you plain text for a form field, Delete removes it — that last one is the record of what you actually sent, so think before using it.

Jobs

  1. New, then paste the posting into the description box — or use one-click capture.
  2. Analyse fit reads it against your master.
  3. You get a score, a short rationale, keywords worth mirroring, and a gap list.
  4. Build a resume from this creates a template with the recommended entries and a tailored summary, and drops you in the Builder.
  5. Draft cover letter gives you three or four paragraphs to edit.
The gap list is the most useful part. "Wants NetSuite, you have nothing on NetSuite" tells you what to add to your master — or that this one isn't worth an application.

The Status dropdown (considering / applied / interviewing / closed) is your application log. Keep it current and after thirty applications you'll know which framing actually gets replies.

Anything the cover letter couldn't support from your master gets listed under flags. Read those before sending.

One-click capture

Grabs a job posting from the page you're looking at, with no copy-paste.

  1. Show your bookmarks bar: Ctrl + Shift + B
  2. Jobs tab → One-click capture
  3. Drag the green "Grab this job" button up onto your bookmarks bar. Don't click it — dragging is the whole point.
  4. Open any job posting: LinkedIn, Indeed, a company careers page
  5. Click the bookmark. A tab opens here with the posting already loaded as a new job.
  6. Check the company and role it guessed, then Analyse fit.

It reads the page your browser already loaded and passes the text in the URL fragment, which never reaches any server. Nothing is scraped, which is why it works on sites that block automated fetching.

If dragging won't work, use Copy the code instead, then create a bookmark by hand and paste that as its URL.

Where your data lives

In career-data — your own private GitHub repo — as four plain JSON files under career/. Every save is a commit, so you have the full history.

You can git clone that repo and read it in a text editor. Nothing here depends on this website continuing to exist, or on any subscription staying paid.

When something breaks

"Your data repo is not connected"Environment variables missing. Vercel → Deployments → ⋯ → Redeploy.
"GitHub rejected the token (401)"Token expired — they last a year. Make a new one, update GITHUB_DATA_TOKEN, redeploy.
"lacks Contents: Read and write"Token permission. Edit it → Repository permissions → Contents → Read and write.
"Changed somewhere else"Two tabs open. Reload the one you care about least, then redo the edit.
"No Anthropic API key"Only the AI buttons need it. Add ANTHROPIC_API_KEY in Vercel and redeploy.
Bounced to loginSession expired. Normal — sign in again.

Anything not on this list, or an error that doesn't name its own fix: send me the exact wording.
